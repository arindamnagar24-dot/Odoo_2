# ENERTRACK

Part 1 – Final Problem Statement
Title: Development of an AI-Powered Integrated ERP System for the Renewable Energy Industry Using Odoo 19

Renewable energy businesses face challenges in managing customer relationships, project execution, inventory, procurement, finances and energy performance through disconnected systems and manual processes. These inefficiencies can increase operational costs, delay projects and limit effective decision-making. Furthermore, businesses often lack a unified platform to analyse energy consumption, evaluate solar investments and monitor environmental impact. The proposed solution, ENERTRACK ERP, is an integrated, AI-powered enterprise resource planning system developed using the Odoo 19 framework. It combines essential business operations with renewable-energy analytics, solar investment estimation, sustainability tracking and intelligent recommendations. By connecting business management with energy intelligence, ENERTRACK aims to streamline workflows, optimise resources, improve operational efficiency and support informed, sustainable business decisions.

Part 2 – Basic Explanation of the Project
ENERTRACK ERP is a specialised ERP platform designed for businesses operating in the renewable-energy sector, particularly:
- Solar installation companies.
- Renewable-energy equipment suppliers.
- Energy consultants and auditors.
- Solar maintenance providers.
- Sustainability service providers.
- Commercial organisations managing their energy consumption.
The platform connects two important areas:

1. Business management: Customers, sales, quotations, finance, inventory, procurement, employees and projects.

2. Energy intelligence: Energy consumption, solar generation, savings estimates, investment calculations, sustainability reporting and intelligent recommendations.

The key difference is that ENERTRACK does not simply monitor energy. It connects energy information with the actual business operations responsible for delivering renewable-energy solutions.
Example of how it works
A customer approaches a solar installation company.
1. The company registers the customer in CRM.
2. The energy assessment module estimates the customer's solar requirements.
3. The sales module prepares a quotation.
4. Once accepted, a project is created.
5. Inventory checks whether the required solar panels, batteries and inverters are available.
6. Procurement handles missing materials.
7. Project managers assign employees and track installation progress.
8. Finance records expenses, invoices and payments.
9. Energy analytics monitors recorded or simulated energy-generation data.
10. ENERTRACK Intelligence analyses the information and suggests possible improvements.
All these activities are connected through one ERP environment.
